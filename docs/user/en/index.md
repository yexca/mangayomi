## Product Principles

- Your files stay where they are. Mangayomi reads them and never renames,
  moves, or rewrites them.
- The only way Mangayomi removes a file is a deletion you review and confirm
  in two steps.
- The folder layout you already use decides how works are grouped.
- Reading position follows you across devices signed in to the same instance.
