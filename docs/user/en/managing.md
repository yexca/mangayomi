# Browsing and Managing Works

[English](managing.md) · [简体中文](../zh-Hans/managing.md) · [繁體中文](../zh-Hant/managing.md) · [日本語](../ja/managing.md) · [한국어](../ko/managing.md)

## Pages

- **Home:** **Continue reading** (works in progress, most recent first) and
  **Recently added**.
- **Library:** every work, with search across titles, authors, and series, a
  status filter (**All**, **Unread**, **In progress**, **Completed**), and
  sorting by **Title**, **Recently added**, or **Recently read**.
- **Series:** every series with its cover, author, and how many works are
  read. A series page lists its works in natural order and offers **Start
  reading** or **Continue** for the first unfinished work.
- **Authors:** every author with the number of series and works. An author page
  shows their series and their **Standalone works** (works without a series).
- **Work details:** cover, author and series links, progress, pages, size,
  type, library, date added, location on disk, and the previous and next work
  in the series.

When you have more than one library, a selector on these pages limits them to
one library or shows **All libraries**.

## Read State

- A work with no progress is unread. Opening it in the reader marks it in
  progress; reaching the last page marks it completed.
- **Mark as read** and **Mark as unread** are available on the work page, in
  multi-select, and in the menu of a series or author (**Mark all as read**,
  **Mark all as unread**). Marking unread clears the saved page.

## Multi-Select

Choose **Select** on the Library page, a series page, or an author page, then
tap covers to select them. The action bar shows the count and offers **Select all** (loaded
works), mark as read, mark as unread, and **Delete**. Close the bar to leave
selection mode.

## Deleting Works

Deleting removes files from your storage. It cannot be undone.

You can delete:

- one work, with **Delete** on its page;
- a selection, with **Delete** in the multi-select bar;
- a whole series, with **Delete series…** in the series menu;
- everything by an author, with **Delete all by this author…** in the author
  menu.

Every deletion has two steps:

1. **Review.** A list of every archive and folder that will be deleted, with
   its full path, size, and file count, plus the total. Folders that would be
   left empty (for example the series folder after deleting all its volumes)
   are listed too and removed with them. Choose **Continue** or **Cancel**.
2. **Confirm.** For more than one work, a series, or an author, type `delete`.
   Then choose **Delete permanently**.

Only what the review listed is deleted, even if new files appeared in the
meantime. If a file cannot be deleted, for example because of permissions, the
others are still deleted and the failures are shown. A review expires after ten
minutes.

Removing a library under **Settings** is different: it only removes the library
from Mangayomi and leaves the files alone.
